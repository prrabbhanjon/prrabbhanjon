## 🚀 About Me

Cloud Subject Matter Expert with **13+ years** of hands-on experience designing, deploying, and running carrier-grade Telco Cloud and NFVI platforms. I specialize in **OpenStack**, **Red Hat OpenStack Platform**, **Nokia NCS/CBIS**, **Kubernetes**, and **OpenShift** — taking clouds from bare metal to stable production and keeping them there.

- 🔭 I work on **Telco Cloud deployments, upgrades, migrations, and production support**
- 🌱 Deep in **CNF/VNF onboarding, SDN/SR-IOV networking, and observability at scale**
- 🎥 I create content on **YouTube**, **Medium**, and **Instagram** — sharing real-world cloud, DevOps, and infrastructure knowledge
- 💬 Ask me about **OpenStack, OpenShift, Ceph, Networking, and Day-2 Operations**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I enjoy turning 3 AM incidents into clean root-cause stories and repeatable runbooks
